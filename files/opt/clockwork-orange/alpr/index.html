Heard by radio

How detection works. Bluetooth listens for Flock's Penguin solar battery packs, its accessory service and SoundThinking Raven gunshot detectors. Wi-Fi spots the camera's own Flock-XXXXXX hotspot and Flock-registered MACs. Weak matches are chip-vendor prefixes many ordinary devices share: logged, never alarmed. Most cameras only talk on Wi-Fi briefly, so the camera database is the reliable layer.
Settings
Passive receive only: nothing is sent to a camera. The uConsole's Wi-Fi can't do monitor mode, so camera probe requests aren't visible; a USB adapter with monitor mode would add them. Signatures: flock-you project (firmware-derived) + IEEE OUI registry.
